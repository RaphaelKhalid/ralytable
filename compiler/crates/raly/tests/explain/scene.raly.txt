space Concepts = MAP[8192]
  A value of `Concepts` is a list of 8192 numbers, where every position holds
  either +1 or -1, and two of them are combined by multiplying position
  against position. Nothing about that list means anything on its own; its
  meaning is which other values it was built from.
  Several values can be added together into a single one, which is what `load`
  counts everywhere below. That only works up to a point. Past it, asking for
  one of the items back returns a different item instead, and nothing fails
  while it happens.

  not derived from a formula.
